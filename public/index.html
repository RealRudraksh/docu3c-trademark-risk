📐 Risk Methodology (Rule-Based, TMEP-Grounded)

Risk is assigned by combining two signals per record — never by asking an LLM "is this HIGH or LOW?":

Signal Weight Description
Token (Jaccard) Similarity 60% Shared word tokens between target and candidate mark
Phonetic / Dominant-Word Overlap 40% How many of TEAR, POUR, LIVE, MORE appear in candidate mark
Risk Level Trigger Conditions Primary TMEP
HIGH Active mark + shared class + combined similarity ≥ 50% § 1207.01, § 1207.01(b)(viii), § 1207.01(d)(i)
MEDIUM-HIGH Active mark + shared class + similarity 25–50% § 1207.01, § 1207.01(a)(v), § 1207.01(d)(i)
MEDIUM Shared class + low sim, OR no class + high sim § 1207.01(a)(v), § 1207.01(b)(iv)
MEDIUM-LOW Abandoned/Cancelled + some overlap § 1207.01(d)(iv)
LOW No class overlap + combined similarity < 20% N/A — distinct mark

⚠️ Uncertainty handling: When confidence is below threshold or evidence is borderline, the system flags for attorney review rather than assigning a confident label. Abandoned marks are not cited as direct conflicts per TMEP § 1207.01(d)(iv) but contribute to crowded-field analysis.
